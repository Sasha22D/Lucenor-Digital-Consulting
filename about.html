Our story

Built on a simple belief

LUCENOR was founded in 2013 with one conviction: most digital transformation projects fail not because of technology, but because strategy, design, and engineering are treated as separate disciplines. We were built to bridge that gap.

Over the past decade we have grown from a small strategy boutique into a full-service digital consultancy — without ever losing the rigour, honesty, and hands-on ownership that defined us from day one.

Today our team of 18 consultants works across strategy, data, technology, design, and cybersecurity — always as one integrated team, never in silos.

What we stand for

Our values

These are not aspirational posters on a wall — they are the principles we use to make decisions every day, on every engagement.

  1. 01

    Clarity over complexity

    We strip away jargon and noise. Every recommendation we make can be explained in plain language — if it can’t, we don’t believe in it yet.

  2. 02

    Outcomes, not outputs

    Deliverables are a means to an end. We measure our success by the business results our clients achieve, not by the volume of slides or code we produce.

  3. 03

    Radical honesty

    We tell clients what they need to hear, not what they want to hear. This builds the trust that makes long-term partnerships possible.

  4. 04

    End-to-end ownership

    We see engagements through from first workshop to live deployment. Handing off a strategy document and walking away is not in our vocabulary.

  5. 05

    Continuous learning

    The digital landscape changes faster than any playbook. We invest heavily in research, training, and experimentation so our clients benefit from what’s actually current.

How we work

Our engagement model

Every engagement is different, but our way of working is consistent — collaborative, rigorous, and relentlessly focused on your outcomes.

Discover

We start by understanding your business, your users, and your constraints — not by proposing solutions before we understand the problem.

Design

Strategy, architecture, and experience design developed iteratively — with your team, not delivered to them in a slide deck.

Deliver

Hands-on implementation alongside your engineers, or full delivery ownership — whichever model fits your team’s capacity and maturity.

Measure

We define success metrics before we start, track them throughout, and hold ourselves accountable for the results — not just the deliverables.
