Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how LUCENOR uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognise your device on subsequent visits and can store preferences or usage information. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixels.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

CategoryPurposeConsent required
Strictly necessary Enable core site functionality (e.g. cookie consent preference storage, session management). Cannot be disabled. No
Analytics Measure audience and usage patterns to help us improve the site (e.g. page views, traffic sources). Data is aggregated and anonymised where possible. Yes
Preferences Remember your settings such as language or theme preference. Yes

We do not currently use advertising or targeting cookies.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by third-party services we use, such as analytics providers. These third parties have their own privacy policies governing cookie use, over which LUCENOR has no control.

4. Cookie Duration

  • Session cookies — expire when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies — remain on your device for a set period (typically up to 13 months) or until you delete them.

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can manage or withdraw your consent to non-essential cookies at any time by:

  • Using the cookie preference centre accessible from the banner displayed on your first visit.
  • Configuring your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Note that blocking all cookies may affect the functionality of our site.

Common browser settings for managing cookies:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above.
