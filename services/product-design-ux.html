What we do

Design that puts users first

Great design is not decoration — it’s the difference between a product people love and one they abandon. We bring rigour, empathy, and craft to every engagement.

User Research & Testing

Interviews, surveys, usability tests, and contextual enquiry — giving your team a deep, evidence-based understanding of your users’ goals, frustrations, and mental models.

Information Architecture

Structuring content and navigation so users always know where they are, where to go next, and how to find what they need — regardless of the complexity of the system.

Interaction Design

Designing the micro-interactions, flows, and states that make digital products feel responsive, predictable, and delightful to use at every touchpoint.

Design Systems

Building scalable component libraries and design tokens that ensure visual consistency across products, accelerate development, and reduce design debt over time.

Accessibility (WCAG)

Auditing and remediating digital products to meet WCAG 2.2 AA standards — ensuring your product is usable by everyone and compliant with accessibility legislation.

Prototyping & Validation

Rapid low- and high-fidelity prototypes tested with real users — de-risking product decisions before a single line of code is written.

Our Approach

How we work

  1. 01

    Discover

    User interviews, stakeholder workshops, and competitive analysis to understand the problem space, user needs, and the constraints that will shape the solution.

  2. 02

    Define

    Synthesising research into personas, journey maps, and design principles — creating a shared foundation that aligns product, engineering, and business stakeholders.

  3. 03

    Design

    Iterative wireframing, prototyping, and visual design — tested continuously with users to validate assumptions and refine the experience before handing off to engineering.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Production-ready specs, a documented design system, and embedded design support during development — ensuring the built product matches the intended experience.
